Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026

Clipwire is a personal tool run by one operator to post short clips to the operator's own social media accounts. This policy explains what data it handles and how.

Accounts you connect

When the operator connects a TikTok, YouTube or Instagram account, Clipwire receives OAuth access tokens from that platform. It uses them only to:

  • read basic account information (account ID, name or handle) to show which account is connected;
  • upload videos and their titles, captions and tags to that account;
  • check the status of those uploads and read back the link to the published post.

Tokens are stored on the operator's own machine or server, in files only the operator can read. They are never sold, shared or sent anywhere except back to the platform that issued them.

Stream content

Clipwire processes publicly available live streams, stream recordings and public chat messages from Twitch and Kick to find highlights. Chat messages are used only to measure activity around a moment. They are kept with the recording for tuning and deleted on a rolling schedule. Clipwire does not build profiles of chat users.

What Clipwire does not do

  • No sign-ups, no user accounts, no tracking cookies, no analytics, no advertising.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data with third parties.
  • No access to direct messages, contacts, or private videos.

Services used

Clipwire uses the YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube account you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. TikTok and Instagram data is handled under the TikTok Privacy Policy and the Meta Privacy Policy.

Removing access and data

  • YouTube / Google: remove Clipwire at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions.
  • Instagram / Facebook: Settings → Apps and websites, or Business Integrations on Facebook.

Revoking access stops Clipwire from using the account straight away. Stored tokens and data are deleted within 30 days of a request, or right away when the account is disconnected. To ask for deletion, or with any question, write to streanerclipsandmore24@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new effective date.
