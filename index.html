Stream highlights, cut for short video.

Clipwire is a personal tool. It finds standout moments in live streams, cuts them into captioned vertical clips, and posts them to its operator's own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts.

What it does

  • Watches public streams on Twitch and Kick and spots moments where chat, audio and on-screen action spike.
  • Renders 9:16 clips with captions and an on-screen credit to the streamer.
  • Posts clips only for streamers who allow it, through a creator campaign or written permission.
  • Uses each platform's official API, on accounts the operator owns and connected themselves.

Who uses it

Clipwire is run by a single operator for their own accounts. It is not a public service: there are no sign-ups, and it never posts on behalf of anyone else.
